PRO_CTDL_4 - CHUONG TRINH QUAN LI BAN HANG ONLINE (C++ - Windows Console)

Cau truc du lieu & Thuat toan ap dung (Chuong 2, 3, 4, 5):
- Chuong 2: Tim kiem tuyen tinh theo ten hang hoa, Sap xep chon (Selection Sort).
- Chuong 3: Danh sach lien ket don (DanhSachLienKet) luu Tai khoan Admin va Chi tiet don hang.
- Chuong 4: Hang doi FIFO (HangDoi) luu danh sach Don hang cho xu li (dat truoc xu li truoc).
- Chuong 5: Cay nhi phan tim kiem (CayHangHoa - BST theo Ma hang) luu danh sach Hang hoa.

Bien dich (MinGW / MSYS2 g++):
    g++ -std=c++11 -Wall -Wextra -o main.exe main.cpp

Chay chuong trinh:
- Dat HangHoa.txt, DonHang.txt, Admin.txt cung thu muc voi main.exe.
- Tai khoan dang nhap mau (trong Admin.txt):
    user1 / 12345678
    admin / admin123
